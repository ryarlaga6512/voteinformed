Why I built this site — and why it matters that you're here

Most people don't follow politics because nobody ever made it accessible to them. The news assumes you already know the backstory. The debates assume you already have a side. I built VoteInformed because I kept seeing smart, thoughtful people feel completely lost when it came to civic life — not because they didn't care, but because the information was never designed for them.

Ryan Yarlagadda June 2026 Media

The problem isn't biased news. It's that people don't know they're reading it.

Every outlet has a perspective. That's not inherently bad — it's inevitable. The real problem is that most people consume news from one or two sources and assume those sources represent the full picture. Media literacy isn't about finding some mythical unbiased source. It's about understanding where your information comes from and what it might be leaving out.

Ryan Yarlagadda June 2026 Voting

Young people don't vote because they feel like it doesn't matter. Here's why that's exactly backwards.

Voter turnout among 18–29 year olds consistently lags behind every other age group. The most common reason isn't apathy — it's a feeling that one vote doesn't change anything. But elections at every level are decided by margins that a single motivated group of young voters could swing. Local races, school boards, state legislatures — these shape daily life far more directly than most people realize.
