Key 2026 dates
01
General Election — November 3, 2026
Polls are open 7am–8pm statewide. California also held its statewide primary on June 2, 2026.
02
Registration deadline — October 19, 2026
Online, by mail (postmarked), or in person — 15 days before Election Day.
03
Same-day registration
Missed the deadline? You can still register and cast a conditional ballot in person through Election Day at any county elections office or vote center.
04
Every voter gets a mail ballot
Ballots go out to all active registered voters ~29 days before Election Day. You can mail it back, drop it in a secure drop box, or still vote in person instead.
Find your polling place

Most of California — including Sacramento, Placer, and El Dorado counties — has moved to the Voter's Choice Act model: instead of one assigned precinct, you can vote at any open vote center in your county, starting several days before Election Day.

Who's on your ballot

California uses a "top-two" primary for most state and federal offices (U.S. Senate, U.S. House, Governor, and other statewide and legislative races) — the two highest vote-getters in June advance to November regardless of party. Exactly who appears on your ballot depends on your specific address, district lines, and any local races or measures — use a personalized lookup rather than a generic candidate list.
