How to vote
Full guide
01
Check if you're registered
Visit vote.gov or your state's Secretary of State website. Takes 60 seconds.
02
Register or update your address
Most states let you register online. Deadlines vary — some allow same-day, others cut off 30 days before.
03
Find your polling place
Vote.org's locator works for every state and tells you your exact location. California voters can also use our California guide.
04
Know what ID you need
Requirements vary by state — some require photo ID, others accept a utility bill. Check before election day.
