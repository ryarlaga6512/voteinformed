About VoteInformed

Built for the people who never got the memo.

"Everyone deserves to know who and what they're voting for — not just people who already follow politics."

Why this exists

Most political content is written for people who are already engaged. The explainers assume you know the backstory. The debate coverage assumes you already have a side. The voter guides are buried on government websites that nobody finds until the night before an election.

VoteInformed was built to fix that. The goal is simple: take everything that feels complicated or partisan about civic life and make it genuinely accessible — to anyone, regardless of how much they follow the news.

An informed vote isn't a Democratic vote or a Republican vote. It's a vote made with real understanding of what's at stake. That's what this site is for.

What we believe

No spin
Every issue is presented with the strongest arguments on both sides. We don't tell you what to think.
Plain language
No jargon, no assumed knowledge. If you need to Google a word to understand the explainer, we've failed.
No ads
This site has no advertisers and no political sponsors. Nobody is paying to have their message here.
Built for everyone
First-time voters, debate students, lifelong citizens — this resource is for anyone who wants to be more informed.
